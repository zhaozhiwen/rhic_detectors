## Closed
- **#16 TOF MRPC pad width and glass (closed 2026-11-16).** Was: pad 3.3 cm ([schambach2006-star-tof-electronics-note](../../raw/schambach2006-star-tof-electronics-note.md)) vs 3.5 cm ([llope2012-star-mrpc-systems-note](../../raw/llope2012-star-mrpc-systems-note.md)); outer/inner glass 0.7/0.55 mm vs 1.1/0.54 mm. [kajimoto2009-star-tof-thesis-note](../../raw/kajimoto2009-star-tof-thesis-note.md) (Fig. 3.4, reproduced from the 2004 TOF proposal) gives **pad width 3.15 cm with a 0.3 cm interval** (pitch 3.45 cm, so Llope's 3.5 cm is the pitch to rounding [note]) and **outer 1.1 mm / inner 0.54 mm glass**, agreeing with Llope 2012. The 2006 design values (3.3 cm; 0.7/0.55 mm) are supported by no other source and are treated as superseded; the 2006 paper does not explain them. Pad length 6.1 cm (Llope 2012, Schambach 2006, thesis Eq. 4.2). Gap count split off as #21.
